title: Sign-in methods
description: How people sign in to a self-hosted instance and how to help them back into their account.
---

People create an account with a username and a password, and sign in with them. The instance never asks for an email address and never sends email.

When someone forgets their password, they use their recovery kit. The app shows the kit when the account is created, and people can make a new one in their account settings. Using the kit sets a new password, signs the account out everywhere and gives the person a new kit.

## Lost passwords

You can create a reset link for someone who has lost their password and their recovery kit. The "Create Password Reset Link" button on the account's page in the admin panel does the same.

```sh
rexctl users reset-link alex
```

The link works once and expires after an hour, and anyone who has it can set a new password for the account. Send it to the person over a channel you trust. Creating the link deletes their recovery kit, and they can make a new one in their account settings.

If you lose access to your own admin account, `rexctl users reset-link` works on the machine without signing in.

## More settings

The "Sign-in & new accounts" card on the "Instance Config" page has the other settings for new accounts, such as letting people start with just a display name and deleting accounts that have no way to sign back in.
